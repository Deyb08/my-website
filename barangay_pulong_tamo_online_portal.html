About Barangay Pulong Tamo

ZIP Code: 3010

Barangay Pulong Tamo is one of the vibrant agricultural and residential barangays situated in the Municipality of San Ildefonso, Bulacan. Divided into 7 distinct Puroks (Purok 1 to Purok 7), the barangay leadership is committed to responsive public service, digital innovation, and community peace and order.
